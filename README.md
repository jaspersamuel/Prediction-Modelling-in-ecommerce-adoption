# Prediction-Modelling-in-ecommerce-adoption

The study aims to address critical gaps in understanding the adoption patterns of e-commerce 
in the UK by: 
• Exploring e-commerce adoption levels across various industries. 
• Identifying key factors influencing adoption, such as broadband connectivity, business 
size, and operational challenges. 
• Developing predictive models to forecast industry-specific and total e-commerce sales 
over a five-year period (2021–2025). 
• Providing strategic recommendations to enhance adoption rates and overcome barriers. 
A mixed-methods approach was employed, integrating quantitative data analysis with 
qualitative insights. Data was sourced from the UK Digital Economy Survey conducted by the 
Office of National Statistics, covering variables such as e-commerce sales, broadband speed, 
and business size. Analytical techniques included descriptive statistics, linear regression, and 
scenario-based forecasting, sales-purchase ratio and correlation analysis.
